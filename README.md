# Jérémy Hugentobler

Welcome to my profile! I am a Computer Science graduate student at **EPFL** specializing in signals, images, and interfaces. My work focuses on **computer vision**, **3D reconstruction**, and **spatial computing**, developing robust computational methods to perceive, understand, and model the physical world.

---

### Focus & Research Interests

- **3D Vision & Geometry Processing**: Neural representations, CAD reverse engineering, surface reconstruction, and point cloud understanding.
- **Computer Graphics**: Exploring mathematical models and computational foundations to accurately represent, simulate, and render the physical world.
- **Applied Machine Learning**: Solving concrete real-world problems by integrating and learning from diverse, multimodal data streams.
- **End-to-End System Design**: Building robust pipelines that integrate data, algorithms, and interactive interfaces into complete products.

---

### Selected Highlights & Projects

  - Researched state-of-the-art 3D CAD generative architectures on high-precision mechanical components.
